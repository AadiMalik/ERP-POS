# Getting Started

## Logging In

Every user signs in with an email and password at the login page. If you forget
your password, use **Forgot Password** on that screen: enter your email. If that
email is not registered, you will see an error and no code is sent. If it is
registered, a 6-digit code is emailed from Dukanaz (it addresses you by name and
expires in 10 minutes). Enter that code and choose a new password. If your
account has **"must change password"** flagged (common for newly created staff
accounts), you'll be asked to set a new password before you can continue.

Customers signing up, signing in with a code, or resetting a password from your
**website** or **mobile app** get that code in an email that uses your business
name, logo, and website colors, with **Powered by Dukanaz** only at the bottom.
For password reset, the email must already be registered as a customer of that
business.

After logging in you land on your **Dashboard**, which summarizes sales, purchases,
and recent activity for your business. Finance widgets (Net Profit, account
summary, and related charts) appear only when your package includes Accounting.
Super Admin accounts (the platform operator) instead land on the Subscription
dashboard, since they manage tenant businesses rather than day-to-day operations.

## How the System Is Organized

- **Business** — your company/tenant account. Everything you do happens inside your
  business; other businesses on the same platform can never see your data.
- **Branches** — physical locations or outlets under your business. Warehouses,
  POS registers, and staff are usually tied to a specific branch.
- **Package/Subscription** — the plan your business is subscribed to determines
  which modules are available (POS, Inventory, Accounting, HRM, Payroll, Service
  Management) and any usage limits (e.g. maximum branches or products). See
  [Subscription & Billing](11-subscription-billing.md).
- **Users & Roles** — every person who logs in is a user with a role that
  determines what they can see and do. See
  [Roles, Permissions & Team Access](02-roles-permissions.md).

## First Steps for a New Business

1. Review **Settings** to configure your business profile, tax settings, print/receipt
   layout, and notification preferences (see [Settings](10-settings.md)).
2. Add your **Branches** if you operate more than one location.
3. Set up your **Chart of Accounts** if you'll use Accounting (a default chart can be
   applied from Accounting Settings).
4. Add **Warehouses**, **Categories**, **Units**, and **Products** if you sell
   physical goods (see [Inventory & Warehouses](05-inventory.md)).
5. Create **Roles** for your team and add **Users** (see
   [Roles, Permissions & Team Access](02-roles-permissions.md)).
6. Open a **POS Register** and start taking orders (see
   [Sales & Point of Sale](03-sales-pos.md)).

## Global Search

The search box in the top bar searches across suppliers, products, orders, purchases
and more at once — each result group only appears if you have permission to view
that area.

## Notifications

The bell icon in the top bar shows in-app alerts (e.g. low stock, pending approvals,
subscription reminders). Configure which alerts you receive under
[Settings](10-settings.md).
